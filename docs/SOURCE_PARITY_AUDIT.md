## How to read this audit

The 25 legacy pages were structurally inspected for document title, heading outline, code/pre markers, and obvious buttons/tabs/forms. These markers show where examples and interactive controls exist and help plan migration. They are not a line-by-line review of all prose, hidden answers, scripts, diagrams or state transitions. Only the Week 14–15 topics listed below received a focused concept comparison against the selected Notion sections and legacy pages.

A destination means that a bilingual structured lesson now exists and can be reached through the content registry; it does **not** mean every source element has been reproduced or approved. All lessons remain in `review`. Keep the original HTML in place until full source, translation, behavior and editorial checks are completed.
